# Role Catalog Entry — IT Support Analyst

**Role ID:** ROLE-IT-SUPPORT-ANALYST  
**Business owner:** Tyler Mackelroy  
**Role group:** `GG-IT-Support-Analysts`

## Purpose
Provide approved user-support capabilities while separating them from broad directory or privileged administration.

## Implemented Entitlement
- AD Reset Password for standard Corporate and Technology workforce users.

## Deferred Entitlements
- Account unlock
- Scoped directory read
- Endpoint-management support
- ServiceNow Analyst

## Exclusions
Domain Admin, DC administration, privileged-account administration, GPO administration, unrestricted server administration, and unrelated group administration.

## Review
Remove membership when the user leaves IT Support, leaves the organization, or no longer requires the business capability.
