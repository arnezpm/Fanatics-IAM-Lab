## Business Trigger
IT Support access had been provisioned inconsistently. Tyler requested a repeatable role model allowing analysts to perform approved account-support functions without broad directory privilege.

## Scope
### In scope
- IT Support Analyst role definition
- Group-based password-reset entitlement
- Standard Corporate and Technology user population
- Non-admin operator testing
- Negative and pressure testing
- Remediation of excessive authorization scope

### Explicitly excluded
- Domain Admin
- Domain Controller administration
- GPO administration
- Unrestricted server administration
- Privileged-account administration
- Group-membership administration
- Account unlock in this case
- Broad user-property write access
- ServiceNow and endpoint administration

## Business Requirement
> IT Support Analysts may reset passwords for standard Corporate and Technology workforce users when required by approved support work. The entitlement must not authorize privileged identities or unrelated AD administration.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Entitlement is assigned through role/group membership, not a direct user ACL.
2. Maya can reset Daniel from a normal support workstation.
3. Maya cannot modify Daniel's group memberships.
4. Maya cannot reset privileged identities outside the standard workforce boundary.
5. No Domain Admin or DC interactive logon is required for the operator.
6. Passwords are never recorded.
7. Positive, negative, and pressure tests are documented.
8. Findings are remediated and retested.

## Risk Rationale
Password reset is a sensitive administrative capability because it changes an authenticator for another identity. If it extends to privileged accounts, a support role may gain an unintended path to control or disrupt a high-impact identity.
